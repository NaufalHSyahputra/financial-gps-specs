## Protocol and evidence

Recruit people matching each persona, including the founder as a participant but not a substitute for all personas. Use realistic, fictional amounts; do not collect account credentials or unnecessary personal financial information. Ask participants to think aloud, avoid explaining terminology before the task, and record completion, errors, explanations, time where useful, and accessibility/platform constraints. Ask participants what they expected, not whether they like the design.

For each session record date, participant pseudonym and relevant persona, tested artifact/version, task, observations, representative paraphrases, and limitations. Store consent and sensitive information outside this repository if needed. Aggregate findings with participant count, conflicting observations, confidence/limitations, and linked assumptions. Log proposed changes in [decisions](decisions.md), distinguishing research finding, product decision and approved baseline change. No target sample size or passing threshold is presumed; agree these with the product owner before interpreting results.
