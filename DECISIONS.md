# Decisions and Open Questions

This is a concise index of consequential existing decisions and unresolved work, not a complete extraction of formulas or a claim that anything is implemented or tested. The living documents are the active baseline; linked archived specifications preserve historical details. Dates and approval status below reflect the source records; where a source states no rationale, the reason is a brief interpretation of its stated behavior, not a newly approved decision. Do not treat an open technical proposal as a settled product rule.

## Owner-frozen and approved decisions

### Product and financial boundaries

- **Actual versus forecast:** Expected income is forecast-only until explicitly linked to actual income and received; current safe-to-spend uses actual money. **Why:** avoid presenting uncertain future money as spendable now. [Scope](docs/archive/product/03-v1-scope.md#2-explicit-v1-boundaries); [product principles](docs/archive/README.pre-migration.md#core-product-principles).
- **Allocation and budgets:** Allocation is explicit; unallocated money does not contribute to safe-to-spend. A cycle budget is a spending plan, not a reservation, and may exceed Flexible with a warning. **Why:** preserve user intent and distinguish planning from money already set aside. [Scope](docs/archive/product/03-v1-scope.md#2-explicit-v1-boundaries).
- **Cycle and forecast:** Safe-to-spend uses the active cycle; forecast handles later obligations and must not mutate actual state. Stable expected income enters the base forecast; uncertain income is scenario-only. **Why:** keep current guidance separate from future assumptions. [Scope](docs/archive/product/03-v1-scope.md#2-explicit-v1-boundaries).
- **Budget lifecycle:** Budgets are cycle-scoped and do not financially roll over; a previous-cycle template creates new budgets with zero spent. Persistent balances, liabilities, allocations and obligations do not reset with the cycle. **Why:** prevent a new plan from rewriting financial facts. [Scope](docs/archive/product/03-v1-scope.md#3-budget-lifecycle-boundary).
- **V1 exclusions:** No automatic bank sync, multi-currency consolidation, shared ledger, probabilistic forecast, conversational LLM advisor or autonomous money movement; see the source for the full list. **Why:** bound v1 and keep guidance deterministic. [Scope](docs/archive/product/03-v1-scope.md#5-out-of-scope).

### Architecture and calendar baseline

- **Technology-agnostic modular monolith (updated 2026-11-18):** Language, UI toolkit, database engine and target platforms are intentionally decoupled from the specification so v1 can be built in any stack. Preserve the single-process, local-first modular-monolith topology and ACID transactional boundaries. **Why:** retain financial integrity without tying learning or implementation to a particular stack. The earlier technology and platform baseline is preserved in the [architecture constitution](docs/archive/architecture/ARCHITECTURE_CONSTITUTION.md#6-platform-and-technology-baseline--locked-v1).
- **Deterministic financial core and integrity:** Separate actual and forecast state, use deterministic calculations, and maintain critical financial consistency atomically; audit history is not event sourcing. **Why:** results must be explainable, reproducible and consistent after corrections. [Constitution §§10–13](docs/archive/architecture/ARCHITECTURE_CONSTITUTION.md#10-financial-state-model--locked-v1); [audit](docs/archive/architecture/ARCHITECTURE_CONSTITUTION.md#17-audit-history-without-event-sourcing--locked-v1).
- **Financial Calendar:** Cycle boundaries are derived from configuration and local dates, not stored as independent canonical cycles. Weekday adjustment and protected active-cycle transitions follow the calendar rules; pre-history is explicitly unavailable. **Why:** produce reproducible boundaries without rewriting the active cycle or inventing history. [Calendar decisions TD-CAL-001–048](docs/archive/architecture/financial-calendar-tactical-ddd-v1.0.md#19-decision-register--td-cal-001-through-td-cal-048); [scope](docs/archive/product/03-v1-scope.md#2-explicit-v1-boundaries).
- **Recorded strategic clarifications:** Existing resolved context and ownership decisions remain baseline until deliberately changed. **Why:** preserve settled domain responsibilities while simplifying their documentation. [Strategic decisions](docs/archive/architecture/strategic-ddd.md#16-resolved-review-clarifications).

### Ledger product decisions

The product owner approved CR-LDG-001–007 on 2026-11-14. The owner froze the consolidated Ledger v1.0 baseline on 2026-11-18, including CR-LDG-008–009, **with findings still open**. This is not independent review, evidence of implementation, passing tests or release readiness. [Decision record](docs/archive/readiness/ledger-product-decisions.md); [freeze record](docs/archive/architecture/financial-accounts-ledger-tactical-ddd-draft.md#32-owner-freeze-record).

- **Account and category history (D-LDG-01–02, 08, 15):** Archive instead of erasing history; account class is not changed in place. Correct a wrong class through a new account and guided, auditable corrections. Category renames and archives preserve references. **Why:** keep historical facts interpretable. [CR-LDG-001–002](docs/archive/readiness/ledger-product-decisions.md).
- **Adjustments and corrections (D-LDG-03–04):** Support explained, audited balance adjustments separately from ordinary income and expense; edits and voids preserve history and recalculate dependents. **Why:** correct actual state without disguising the correction as spending or income. [CR-LDG-001](docs/archive/readiness/ledger-product-decisions.md).
- **Negative balances and payments (D-LDG-05–06):** Allow truthful negative tracked asset balances with a warning, while eligible Available contribution is floored at zero; reject liability payments above recorded outstanding. **Why:** record actual asset activity without inventing unsupported liability credit. [CR-LDG-001](docs/archive/readiness/ledger-product-decisions.md).
- **Refunds (D-LDG-07, 13–14, 16):** Linked refunds reverse attributable eligible spending and cannot exceed the original purchase; unlinked refunds are separate and do not automatically change a budget. Resolve invalidating corrections explicitly. Unsupported positive card credit cannot be recorded by silently dropping part of a refund. **Why:** preserve the full actual event and prevent over-refunding or invented credit. [CR-LDG-001–002](docs/archive/readiness/ledger-product-decisions.md).
- **Historical entry and transfers (D-LDG-09–10, 30):** Permit valid pre-calendar-floor transactions without inventing a cycle; mark affected cycle reports incomplete or unavailable. A transfer may have a separately recognized fee; confirmed linked changes commit together. Reject same-account transfers. **Why:** retain actual history and distinguish movement from spending. [CR-LDG-001](docs/archive/readiness/ledger-product-decisions.md); [CR-LDG-009](docs/archive/readiness/ledger-product-decisions.md#v1-transfer-validation--cr-ldg-009).
- **Linked income correction (D-LDG-11):** Confirm and atomically coordinate corrections to linked income; after unlinking, derive `EXPECTED` or `MISSED` from expected time, and require guided resolution if intervening changes make the result unsafe. **Why:** avoid partial or fabricated lifecycle changes. [CR-LDG-001](docs/archive/readiness/ledger-product-decisions.md).
- **Asset opening snapshots (D-LDG-17–18, 20):** Treat an opening balance as a snapshot at an effective financial time; do not apply earlier transactions to it again or infer earlier balance. Preview audited corrections and ask for meaningful unclear before/after ordering. **Why:** avoid double-counting while retaining historical activity. [CR-LDG-002–004](docs/archive/readiness/ledger-product-decisions.md).
- **Historical card debt (D-LDG-19, 21–27):** Validate payments at their financial time and revalidate affected history. Earlier known debt is a checkpoint, not additional debt; discrepancies or insufficient history remain visible and unresolved until corrected. Require user input when meaningful ordering is unknown; never partially save invalid changes. **Why:** prevent invented debt, concealed credit and double-counting. [CR-LDG-003–007](docs/archive/readiness/ledger-product-decisions.md#correction-decisions--cr-ldg-003).
- **Equal-time ordering (D-LDG-28–29):** When equal timestamps make order financially significant, obtain and preserve the user's real ordering, revalidate it after relevant changes and surface indeterminate results if it cannot be established. Do not fabricate timestamps. **Why:** make order-dependent results deterministic without guessing. [CR-LDG-008](docs/archive/readiness/ledger-product-decisions.md#equal-time-ordering--cr-ldg-008).

## OPEN — unresolved questions and verification

These items remain open even where a product outcome or the Ledger baseline is frozen. Resolve the specific rule or contract before implementing an affected path; verification findings require evidence, not only a decision.

- **OPEN — Expense allocation drawdown (Q-05 / AUD-LDG-003):** `RULE-BUD-105` says an expense paid from Flexible-funded money reduces `FlexibleMoney`, subject to reconciliation. Ledger §13.4 and `RULE-ALLOC-102` describe a truthful expense reducing `AvailableMoney` while explicit allocations remain unchanged, potentially causing `ALLOCATION_SHORTFALL`. Decide how an expense draws down Flexible versus Reserved, including when allocations remain unchanged and when shortfall applies, before coding the expense slice. Do not assume an automatic release of Reserved. [Financial engine](docs/archive/requirements/04-financial-engine.md#rule-bud-105-expense-accounting-interaction); [Ledger example](docs/archive/architecture/financial-accounts-ledger-tactical-ddd-draft.md#134-truthful-shortfall); [consistency findings](docs/archive/architecture/financial-accounts-ledger-tactical-ddd-draft.md#30-review-disposition-and-handoff).

The owner-frozen candidate is not a completed implementation contract for every path. [Question register](docs/archive/readiness/decisions.md); [current findings](docs/archive/architecture/financial-accounts-ledger-tactical-ddd-draft.md#30-review-disposition-and-handoff).
- **OPEN — Historical liability and ordering (AUD-LDG-002, 004):** Review liability opening provenance and completeness, checkpoint interval boundaries, exact as-of semantics and invalidation of stale ordering choices.
